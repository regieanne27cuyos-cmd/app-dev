# My Favorite Series: *Bleach*

## 🌀 Overview
*Bleach* is a Japanese anime and manga series created by **Tite Kubo**.  
It follows **Ichigo Kurosaki**, a teenager who accidentally gains the powers of a **Soul Reaper** — a guardian who protects humans from evil spirits and guides lost souls to the afterlife.

Bleach explores themes of **duty, friendship, sacrifice, and inner strength**, wrapped in intense battles and supernatural elements.

## ⚔️ Main Characters
- **Ichigo Kurosaki** – The main protagonist who becomes a substitute Soul Reaper.  
- **Rukia Kuchiki** – A Soul Reaper who gives Ichigo her powers.  
- **Orihime Inoue** – Ichigo’s kind-hearted friend with healing powers.  
- **Uryu Ishida** – A Quincy archer who hunts Hollows.  
- **Renji Abarai** – Rukia’s childhood friend and a lieutenant in the Soul Society.

- I love bleach for its Epic sword fights and unique abilities.
